The AI logic (transparent on purpose)

Signal
  • • Trend: 20-day EMA vs 50-day EMA
  • • Filter: 14-day RSI (skip overbought/oversold)
  • • Confidence scales with trend strength
  • • Weak setups → no trade
Action
Uptrend → BUY a CALL
Downtrend → BUY a PUT
Otherwise → no trade
Always defined-risk: a bought call or put, ~30 days out, near the money.
